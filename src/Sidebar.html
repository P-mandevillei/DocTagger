Doc Tag Index reads this document's title and managed tag text. When you insert or sync tags, it writes the document title, document and tag links, tag values, identifiers, and sync times to the registry Sheet you choose.

Data stays in Google files and app properties under your control. It is visible to anyone you share the document or registry with. The add-on does not send document content to the developer or advertising, analytics, or AI services.
