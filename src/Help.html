Doc Tag Index adds controlled, visible property tags to the current document and writes an index of those tags to a Google Sheet selected by the user.

Data used

The add-on reads the current document's title and managed tag text. When you synchronize, it stores the title, document and tag links, tag values, internal identifiers, and synchronization times in your chosen registry Sheet. It does not copy surrounding passages.

The developer does not receive document or registry content. Access is controlled by the sharing settings on your Google files.

Manage or delete data

  • Use the sidebar's deletion action to purge the current document's indexed rows.
  • Delete the registry Sheet to remove the entire index.
  • Remove the visible tags normally in Docs if you no longer need them.
  • Uninstall the add-on and revoke its access from your Google Account to stop future access.
