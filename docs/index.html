Structured tags for collaborative documents

DocTagger

Insert controlled property tags in Google Docs™ and synchronize a searchable, countable index to a Google Sheets™ registry that you own.

Define

Create properties such as Status or Topic and define their allowed options.

Tag

Insert visible tags at the cursor so documents remain understandable without the add-on.

Index

Count occurrences and documents, and follow links back to each document or tag.

Privacy-first operation

The add-on works entirely in Google services. It does not send document or registry content to the developer, advertisers, analytics services, or AI systems. Users choose the registry Sheet and control who can access it.

Pricing

DocTagger is free. There are no subscriptions, advertisements, or in-app purchases.
